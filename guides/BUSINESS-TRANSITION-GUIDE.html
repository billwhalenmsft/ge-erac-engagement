Business Transition Guide

For business owners & the program lead

GE Verisk ERAC — Lite CRM · Business Transition Guide

Audience: ERAC business owners (Claims Operations, Actuarial, Legal, Risk) and the sponsoring program lead. Read time: ~10 minutes. Companion docs: TECHNICAL-HANDOFF.md (for your IT / implementation team) and PARTNER-TAKEOVER.md (for a systems integrator such as PwC).

1. What this is

The ERAC Lite CRM is a working, install-ready Microsoft Dynamics 365 / Dataverse application that gives your reinsurance cedent-management teams one shared workspace for the relationships, risk, and engagement work that is today spread across spreadsheets, email, and disconnected systems.

It is delivered as a self-contained, turnkey solution package — no dependency on Microsoft's demo environment and no custom code you can't own. You (or an implementation partner) import it into your own Power Platform environment and it stands up the tables, views, navigation, and app.

It is called "Lite" deliberately: it delivers the highest-value 20% of a full CRM build — cedent 360°, engagement logging, risk and partnership rating, and a portfolio dashboard — as a proof point you can put in front of real users in days, not quarters, and then extend.

2. The business outcomes it delivers

Outcome What changes for the team
One cedent 360° view Every cedent's profile, tier, partnership rating, contacts, and engagement history in one record — no more hunting across files
Engagement log with AI summaries Meetings and touchpoints captured against the cedent, optionally summarized by Copilot
Partnership rating (PRR) dashboard Portfolio-wide view of partnership health, tiering, and rating trend
Risk assessment Structured risk capture per cedent, visible alongside the relationship
Engagement Kanban Work-in-flight across the team on a stage board, not in someone's inbox
Portfolio reporting Power BI-ready views for leadership reporting

The one-liner for leadership: ERAC gets a single, governed system of record for cedent relationships — standing up in your own tenant, owned by you, extensible by you or your partner.

3. What's in the box

  • The installable solution — a managed Power Platform solution (.zip) that creates:
  • Cedent, contact, engagement, and task extensions on standard Dynamics tables
  • Five ERAC-specific tables: Partnership Rating, Risk Assessment, Treaty, Reserve Adequacy, Dispute
  • Pre-built, filtered views (e.g. "ERAC Cedents — Active") and navigation (the ERAC app)
  • Dashboard / analytics web resources
  • Demo/seed data — a set of illustrative cedents and contacts so the app is not empty on day one (replace with your data during rollout).
  • This business guide, a technical handoff, and a partner takeover guide.

Note on "Lite": the Actuarial and Legal deep-dives, treaty/contract integration, and legacy data migration are Phase 2 scope — designed for, but not included in, this package. See §6.

4. How it gets rolled out (the shape, not the steps)

Your IT team or partner follows the technical handoff. From a business standpoint the rollout is four moves:

  1. Install — the package is imported into your Power Platform environment (~30 minutes).
  2. Connect your identity — no maker- or demo-specific settings; your team's sign-in and security roles govern access.
  3. Load your cedents — replace the seed data with your real cedent portfolio.
  4. Go live with a pilot team — one team (e.g. Claims Operations) uses it for real for 2–4 weeks, then you decide what to extend.

Time to a usable pilot: days. No new infrastructure — it runs on the Power Platform you already license with Dynamics 365 / Microsoft 365.

5. What you own vs. what Microsoft provides

You own Microsoft provides (platform)
Your environment, your data, your security model Dynamics 365 / Dataverse / Power Platform
The solution package (yours to extend or hand to a partner) Copilot (optional, for engagement summaries)
The rollout decisions and pilot scope Power BI for reporting

There is no lock-in to a Microsoft-run environment. This is the core design principle of the package: it is genericized so it installs cleanly into your tenant and is maintainable by your people or your chosen partner.

6. Where it goes next (Phase 2 — designed, not yet built)

The Lite CRM is the foundation. The evaluated roadmap:

  • Actuarial workspace — reserving analytics, experience studies, assumption traceability.
  • Legal workspace — treaty contract tracking, legal positions, arbitration dashboard.
  • Contract/treaty integration — read-only treaty/contract data surfaced in CRM views.
  • Dataverse-backed everything — replacing any remaining demo data with governed records.
  • Legacy migration — historical CRM data brought forward.
  • Copilot Studio agent — a natural-language assistant over your live cedent data.

Because ERAC is being evaluated alongside a D365 ERP decision (with PwC), Phase 2 is where the CRM and ERP stories converge — see the partner guide for how a systems integrator takes this turnkey.
